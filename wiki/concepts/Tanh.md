## Summary
[[Tanh]] is an [[ActivationFunction]] that squashes inputs into the range -1 to 1. It adds [[Nonlinearity]] but can saturate for very large positive or negative inputs, reducing useful [[Gradient]] flow.

## Connections
- [[ActivationFunction]] — tanh is a classic activation function.
- [[Sigmoid]] — both are bounded, S-shaped nonlinear functions with saturation risk.
