[[NLP]] (Natural Language Processing) is the AI field focused on modeling, understanding, and generating human language. Language is naturally sequential, so NLP is a major application area for [[SequenceModel]], [[RNN]], [[Attention]], [[Transformer]], and [[LLM]] methods.
