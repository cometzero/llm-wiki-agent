## Definition
Prompt engineering is the practice of writing model inputs that clearly specify the task, constraints, context, examples, and desired response format.

## Key Points
- It relies on the model's [[InstructionFollowing]] ability.
- Clear prompts reduce ambiguity and help the model choose the intended task behavior.
- Prompting complements, but does not replace, [[InstructionTuning]] or [[SupervisedFineTuning]].

## Connections
- Assistant-style LLMs become more responsive to prompt instructions after SFT and preference optimization.
