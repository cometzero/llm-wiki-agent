## Definition
A [[Vector]] is an abstract geometric or algebraic object in a [[VectorSpace]], represented numerically via [[Coordinate]]s.

## Core view
- The vector is the invariant object.
- Its coordinate representation depends on the chosen [[Basis]].

## AI usage
In [[LLM]], token [[Embedding]], [[HiddenState]], and [[Gradient]] are typically stored and transformed as [[Vector]]s.

## Connections
- [[Coordinate]], [[Basis]], [[Matrix]], [[DotProduct]], [[Norm], [Norm]]
- [[Embedding]], [[Attention]], [[Gradient]], [[Regularization]]
