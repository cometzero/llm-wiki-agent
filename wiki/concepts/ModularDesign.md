[[ModularDesign]] refers to breaking a system into swappable, self-contained units so it is easier to manufacture, assemble, service, and scale. In the context of [[AIInfrastructure]], the corpus uses the term to describe the [[VeraRubinNVL72]] compute tray redesign that removes cables and hoses.

## Why It Matters
- Reduces assembly time.
- Lowers the number of failure points.
- Improves serviceability and reliability.
- Makes rack-scale systems easier to mass-produce.
