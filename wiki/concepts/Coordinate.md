## Definition
[[Coordinate]] is the numeric representation of a [[Vector]] under a specific [[Basis]].

## Note
Coordinate is not the vector itself; it is expression of that vector along chosen axes.

## Practical relevance
In [[LLM]] and representation learning, the same conceptual state may admit multiple coordinate expressions depending on feature basis and layer transformations.

## Connections
