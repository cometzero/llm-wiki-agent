## Summary
[[HolisticAdmissions]] refers to student selection based on more than test scores alone, especially through grades, school records, activities, and contextual evaluation. The source recommends this route for many specialized schools and for students who can use record-building to reach above their raw grade band.

## Key Points
- Especially important for specialized schools with strong activity support and recognizable reputations.
- Can allow students to target universities above what a pure grade cut would suggest.
- Still depends on a solid academic baseline and strategic course / activity choices.
