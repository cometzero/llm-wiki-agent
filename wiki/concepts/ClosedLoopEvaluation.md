## Summary
[[ClosedLoopEvaluation]] is the evaluation mode where a driving policy is executed inside an interactive simulator or environment so that feedback, compounding error, and intervention behavior can be observed. The weekly VLA study treats this as more deployment-relevant than open-loop prediction alone.

## Connections
- [[VisionLanguageActionForAutonomousDriving]] — the field needs closed-loop proof to support safety claims.
- [[Safety]] — collisions, fallback behavior, and monitor logic become visible in closed loop.
- [[ActionGrounding]] — grounded actions should remain feasible when the vehicle actually moves.
