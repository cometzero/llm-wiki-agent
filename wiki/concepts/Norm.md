## Definition
A [[Norm]] measures vector magnitude.

## Importance in ML
- Used to express constraint, scale, and stability.
- Appears in penalties and optimization regularization workflows.

## Variants
- [[L1Norm]] and [[L2Norm]] encode different geometric and regularization properties.
