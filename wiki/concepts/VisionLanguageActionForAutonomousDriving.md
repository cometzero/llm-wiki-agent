[[VisionLanguageActionForAutonomousDriving]] refers to multimodal driving systems that connect vision inputs, language context, and action outputs in a closed-loop autonomous driving setting.

## Summary
This concept covers study and evaluation of whether language improves driving policy grounding, safety, and generalization, or merely adds interpretability after the fact. In the corpus, it is treated as a weekly reading and analysis program focused on action grounding, language role, closed-loop evaluation, safety, and long-tail edge cases.
