## Summary
A [[LinearCombination]] combines inputs by multiplying each input by a weight and summing the results, often with a [[Bias]] term: `z = w·x + b`. It is the core calculation behind a [[Perceptron]] and many [[LinearLayer]] operations.

## Connections
- [[Perceptron]] — computes a weighted sum as a linear combination.
- [[LinearLayer]] — implements matrix-based linear combinations.
- [[Weight]] and [[Bias]] — learned parameters used in the calculation.
