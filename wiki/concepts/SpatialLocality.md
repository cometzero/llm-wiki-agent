## Definition
Spatial locality is the property that nearby values in structured data, such as neighboring pixels in an image, tend to be more related than distant values.

## Relevance
CNNs exploit spatial locality through local receptive fields: early layers inspect small neighboring pixel regions before combining them into larger features.
