# Representation Learning

[[RepresentationLearning]] is the practice of transforming raw data into a feature space that is easier for a model to learn from.

## Core Idea
- Good representations can simplify the underlying function approximation problem.
- Learned coordinates often make classes or patterns more separable.
- Representation learning is especially useful in high-dimensional settings.

## Connections
- [[CurseOfDimensionality]] — reduces the effective difficulty of sparse high-dimensional data.
- [[FeatureMatrix]] — often changes what the columns mean.
- [[DimensionalityReduction]] — often overlaps with compressing the learned representation.
