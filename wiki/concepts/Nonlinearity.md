## Summary
[[Nonlinearity]] is the ability of a model to represent relationships that are not limited to straight-line transformations. Neural networks get nonlinearity mainly by applying an [[ActivationFunction]] between [[LinearLayer]] operations.

## Connections
- [[ActivationFunction]] — introduces nonlinear behavior into neural networks.
- [[MultiLayerPerceptron]] — needs nonlinearity to model complex patterns.
- [[UniversalApproximation]] — depends on suitable nonlinear activation functions.
