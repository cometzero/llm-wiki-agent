## Definition
[[TerawattComputing]] refers to AI compute systems and supply chains that target annual compute capacity on the order of [[1,000,000 gigawatt|1TW]].

In this source, it is framed as a civilizational threshold for enabling persistent AI/robot expansion beyond Earth-scale constraints.

## Mechanism
- Reduce time-to-production loops by co-locating semiconductor lifecycle functions (design, memory, lithography masks, testing, packaging).
- Couple chip output with launch-scale logistics powered by [[SpaceX]].
- Shift part of AI deployment to orbit where solar input and cooling constraints can be structurally different.

## Why It Matters
The source argues that AI compute demand can outrun terrestrial expansion, creating a structural need for off-planet compute and manufacturing strategies.
