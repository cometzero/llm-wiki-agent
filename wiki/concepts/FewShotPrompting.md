## Definition
Few-shot prompting gives an [[LLM]] a small number of input-output examples inside the prompt so it can imitate the desired pattern.

## Role in Prompt Engineering
It is a common [[PromptEngineering]] technique when output style or structure matters. The examples act as local guidance without changing model weights.
