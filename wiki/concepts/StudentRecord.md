## Summary
[[StudentRecord]] captures the school-record portfolio used in Korean admissions, including grades, activities, course choices, and detailed qualitative entries. In the current corpus it is treated as something students must actively shape through planning, participation, and teacher communication.

## Key Points
- Matters not only for holistic admissions but increasingly across the broader admissions system.
- Requires proactive activity planning rather than passive reliance on the school.
- Can help students outperform what grades alone would predict.
