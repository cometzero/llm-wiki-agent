## Summary
[[VisuomotorControl]] links perception directly to action, using visual, tactile, and proprioceptive signals to generate motor commands. In the current corpus it is the central pattern behind Helix 02's pixel-to-full-body control and Figure 03's integration of cameras, tactile sensors, and learned policies.

## Key Points
- The relevant inputs include head cameras, palm cameras, tactile sensing, and proprioception.
- The output is not just hand movement but coordinated whole-body action.
- Richer perception reduces brittleness when objects move, become occluded, or require precise force control.
