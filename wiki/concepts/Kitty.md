Kitty is a GPU-accelerated terminal emulator maintained by [[KovidGoyal]]. In [[lwn-weekly-edition-2026-07-16-1081915]], it is covered as a terminal platform with protocol extensions, low-latency rendering, and a “kittens” ecosystem.
