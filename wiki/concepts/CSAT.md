## Summary
[[CSAT]] is the standardized-test pathway discussed in contrast with school-record-driven admissions. In the source it remains important, but the author argues that even test-oriented strategies increasingly interact with grades and school context.

## Key Points
- Some schools still channel students into test-heavy regular admissions strategies.
- The source warns that a pure test-focused path often leads to repeat exam years and high personal risk.
- The relative importance of the exam is framed as changing rather than disappearing.
