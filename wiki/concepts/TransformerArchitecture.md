## Definition
Transformer architecture is the neural network design behind most modern LLMs, built from attention layers, feed-forward layers, residual connections, normalization, and token embeddings.

## Key Points
- It processes token sequences using attention rather than recurrent time steps.
- Its parameter count comes from embedding matrices, attention projections, and feed-forward layers.
- It enables large-scale pretraining, instruction tuning, and preference optimization workflows.

## Connections
- [[ScalingLaw]] is often studied on Transformer language models.
- [[InstructionTuning]] adapts pretrained Transformer models into assistant-style systems.
