## Definition
A Vision Transformer (ViT) is a computer-vision architecture that splits an image into patches and processes those patch embeddings with transformer attention.

## Relevance
Day 17 mentions ViT as a modern alternative to [[ConvolutionalNeuralNetworks]]. Unlike CNNs, ViTs rely on attention across image patches rather than learned convolution kernels, but they still begin from localized image patches.
