## Summary
[[LocoManipulation]] is the robotics problem of moving through the world while manipulating objects at the same time. The Helix 02 source presents it as one of the hardest unsolved problems in humanoid robotics because walking, reaching, carrying, and balancing continually constrain one another.

## Key Points
- Classical systems often split locomotion and manipulation into separate controllers joined by brittle handoffs.
- Full-body learned systems aim to reason about the body as one coupled dynamical system.
- Long-horizon household tasks are used as a benchmark for real competence.
