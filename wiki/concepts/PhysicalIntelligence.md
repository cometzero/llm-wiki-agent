## Summary
[[PhysicalIntelligence]] is the idea that the next major frontier for AI/software is embodied competence in the physical world rather than purely digital applications. In this corpus it is used to frame humanoid robotics as both a technical and platform shift.

## Key Points
- Robots are portrayed as an emerging computing platform.
- Simulation tools and open-source components are lowering barriers to experimentation.
- Figure 03 is offered as a concrete example of progress toward this frontier.
