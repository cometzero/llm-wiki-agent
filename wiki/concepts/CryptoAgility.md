## Summary
[[CryptoAgility|Crypto Agility]] is the ability to move systems between cryptographic algorithms and protocol choices without major redesign. In this issue, it appears in the context of migrating web and OpenPGP infrastructure toward [[PostQuantumCryptography|Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)]].
