## Definition
[[SpaceBasedAIComputing]] is an architecture strategy that places AI compute systems in space to avoid terrestrial bottlenecks in power, land, and iterative capacity expansion.

## Core Assumptions in Source
- Solar availability and thermal profiles can lower long-run energy complexity for selected workloads.
- Orbital logistics plus large payload throughput can support scale-out of compute infrastructure.
- Space-based deployment can become cost-competitive with ground AI in a 2~3 year horizon under specific operational assumptions.

## Key Mechanisms
- Orbital power via sustained insolation and optimized panel orientation.
- Reduced dependency on ground thermal/heat-dissipation constraints for dedicated AI hardware design.
- Integration with large-scale launch systems (e.g., [[Starship]]).
