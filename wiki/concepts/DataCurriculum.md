## Definition
A data curriculum is an ordering, mixture, or staged selection of training data designed to make learning more effective than using an unstructured data stream.

## Key Points
- Curriculum choices can control difficulty, quality, domain balance, and task diversity.
- In LLM training, data curriculum interacts with [[DataScaling]] because quantity alone is not enough.
- Better curricula can improve convergence and final behavior under the same [[ComputeBudget]].

## Connections
- Instruction datasets can be curated as a curriculum for [[InstructionTuning]].
