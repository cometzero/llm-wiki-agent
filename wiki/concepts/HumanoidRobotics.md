## Summary
[[HumanoidRobotics]] refers to robot systems with human-like morphology that must coordinate balance, locomotion, perception, and manipulation in unstructured environments. In this corpus, the field is framed as moving from brittle demos toward longer-horizon, learned, physically grounded autonomy.

## Key Points
- Recent progress is tied to better actuators, sensing, simulation, and learned control.
- A major bottleneck is integrating movement and manipulation into one continuous policy.
- Figure's work is presented as evidence that humanoids may become practical platforms for household and labor tasks.
