## Summary
[[TactileSensing]] is the use of touch information to guide robot interaction with objects. The corpus emphasizes fingertip sensing and palm cameras as the combination that unlocks delicate humanoid tasks such as pill pickup, bottle opening, and controlled syringe use.

## Key Points
- Touch compensates for occlusion and imperfect visual observation.
- Fine force sensing helps robots avoid dropping, crushing, or slipping objects.
- Dexterous humanoid manipulation depends on tightly coupling tactile and visual feedback.
