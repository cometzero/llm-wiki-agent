## Definition

Scaling laws describe empirical relationships between model performance and factors such as model size, dataset size, and compute. They help teams reason about how performance may change as training resources grow.

## Why It Matters

Training-stack decisions often depend on expected scaling behavior. Scaling laws are useful planning tools, but production AI systems still require evaluation, serving analysis, and feedback loops.
