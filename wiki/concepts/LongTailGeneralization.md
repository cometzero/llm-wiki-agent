## Summary
[[LongTailGeneralization]] is the ability of a model to behave correctly in rare, unusual, or hard-to-cover scenarios such as construction zones, emergency vehicles, or unusual pedestrian behavior. The VLA for AD study treats this as a central reason to investigate language-grounded reasoning.
