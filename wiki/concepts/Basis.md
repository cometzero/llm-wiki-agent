## Definition
A [[Basis]] is a set of basis vectors that defines how a [[Vector]] in a [[VectorSpace]] is represented numerically.

## Key claim
Changing basis changes the [[Coordinate]] representation of the same [[Vector]], even though the vector itself is unchanged.

## ML implication
Understanding basis dependence is essential for interpreting dimensionality-reduction and representation changes in learned spaces.
