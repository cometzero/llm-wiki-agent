## Definition

[[Routines]] are event- or schedule-triggered task starters for [[ClaudeCode]] that reduce manual intervention.

## Core Mechanism

- Trigger by webhooks, API events, or schedules.
- Launch autonomous tasks (code checks, maintenance, fixes).
- Provide morning-ready PR states and recurring automation.

## Relation

- Complements [[Autofix]] and [[RemoteControl]].
- Supports [[AsynchronousExecution]] and lower-friction developer workflows.

## Source Signal

The keynote highlights reducing manual start-up overhead for repeated operational tasks through programmable triggering.
