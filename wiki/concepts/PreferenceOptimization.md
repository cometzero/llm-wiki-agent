## Definition
Preference optimization is a post-pretraining alignment stage that adjusts a model toward preferred responses using human or AI preference signals. It follows [[Pretraining]] and often complements instruction tuning.

## Connections
- [[RLAlignment]] — reinforcement-style alignment can use preference or reward signals.
- [[Pretraining]] — preference optimization typically occurs after base model pretraining.
