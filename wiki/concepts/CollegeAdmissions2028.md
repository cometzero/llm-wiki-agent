## Summary
[[CollegeAdmissions2028]] refers to the expected Korean admissions environment around the 2028 intake, especially the growing weight of internal grades, school records, and school-type strategy. The source treats the new system as a driver of high school polarization and more tactical school selection.

## Key Points
- Internal grades and school-record quality are described as mattering across multiple admissions tracks.
- School choice becomes a strategic lever rather than just a prestige signal.
- Students are encouraged to align school type with a realistic admissions pathway.
