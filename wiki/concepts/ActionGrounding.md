## Summary
[[ActionGrounding]] is the idea that language or reasoning must connect to an executable driving output, not merely a description of the scene. In the VLA for AD study, it is the primary test for whether a model is a real policy system or only an explanatory system.

## Connections
- [[VisionLanguageActionForAutonomousDriving]] — the broader field that depends on action grounding.
- [[ClosedLoopEvaluation]] — grounded action should survive interaction, not just offline scoring.
- [[VisionAction]] — direct VA systems already ground to action, but without language.
- [[EndToEndVLA]] — should ground through trajectory or control.
- [[DualSystemVLA]] — should ground language guidance into planner-consumable constraints or meta-actions.
