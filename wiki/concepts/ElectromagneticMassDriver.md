## Definition
[[ElectromagneticMassDriver]] is a launch and acceleration architecture using electromagnetic force to send mass from lunar/low-gravity environments, presented as a future mechanism for scaling to [[Petawatt]]-class systems.

## Source Context
The source presents it as a post-[[TerawattComputing]] stage: after building orbital infrastructure and chip mass flow, mass-driver infrastructure on [[달]] could reduce energy and cost of scaling compute ecosystems to [[Petawatt]] levels.

## Significance
- Removes reliance on conventional single-stage launch bottlenecks.
- Pairs with space solar/thermal and manufacturing assumptions to pursue longer-run AI civilization scaling.
