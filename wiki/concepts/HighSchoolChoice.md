## Summary
[[HighSchoolChoice]] is the strategic decision of selecting a high school based on the admissions system a student expects to face. The current source argues that the dominant criterion should be whether the student can realistically earn top grades and build a strong record, not whether the school has vague prestige.

## Key Points
- The best school is not always the most famous school.
- Top specialized schools can still be advantageous when they have strong holistic-admissions outcomes.
- Mid-tier prestige schools may underperform if competition is high and student support is uneven.
