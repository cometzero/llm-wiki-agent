[[MemoryHierarchy]] is the layered organization of storage from small and fast to large and slow. In the GPU setting it includes [[Registers]], [[SharedMemory]], [[L1Cache]], [[L2Cache]], and [[GlobalMemory]], and it explains why moving data often dominates runtime cost.
