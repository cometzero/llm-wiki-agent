## Summary
[[UniversalApproximation]] refers to the theoretical property that a sufficiently large [[MultiLayerPerceptron]] with suitable [[ActivationFunction]] choices can approximate a wide range of functions. It describes expressive capacity, not a guarantee that training will always succeed.

## Connections
- [[MultiLayerPerceptron]] — the theorem is commonly discussed for MLPs.
- [[Nonlinearity]] — required for the expressive power behind the result.
