## Definition
A [[LinearMap]] (linear transformation) is a function between vector spaces that preserves vector addition and scalar multiplication.

## Relation to [[Matrix]]
A [[Matrix]] is a concrete calculation form of a linear map in coordinates.

## AI examples
- [[DenseLayer]], [[Projection]], and attention Q/K/V projections are commonly represented as linear maps.
- Rank/effective information can be interpreted as the map’s [[Rank]].
