### Theoretical Framework
The progression builds from:
1. **Basic computation** (perceptron, MLP, activation functions) → Day 13
2. **Training mechanics** (forward pass, backpropagation, parameters) → Day 14
3. **Deep representation learning** (layers, initialization, gradient flow) → Day 15
4. **Training stability** (BatchNorm, Dropout, epoch/batch dynamics) → Day 16
5. **Spatial processing** (convolution, pooling, ResNet) → Days 17-18
6. **Sequence modeling** (RNN, BPTT, LSTM/GRU, attention) → Days 19-20
7. **Next: Transformer architecture** → Coming days
