## Summary
[[VisionLanguageActionForAutonomousDriving]] is the research area that combines visual perception, language-grounded reasoning, and executable driving action. In this wiki it serves as the umbrella term for survey material, weekly study notes, and evaluation frameworks around autonomous driving policies that use language in a materially grounded way.
