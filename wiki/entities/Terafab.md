## Summary
[[Terafab]] is an infrastructure initiative described as a vertically integrated, vertically accelerated AI chip and compute manufacturing effort.

It is positioned as a response to AI compute scarcity, arguing that terrestrial expansion alone will not meet future demand at the pace required by advanced AI systems.

## Role in the Wiki
- Central project link for the [[Earth&Space]], [[SpaceBasedAIComputing]], and [[TerawattComputing]] theme.
- Operational axis connecting [[Tesla]], [[SpaceX]], and [[xAI]] as collaborating entities.
- Provides concrete numeric targets for scaling narrative: current annual compute capacity ~[[20 gigawatt|20GW]] vs target [[1,000,000 gigawatt|1TW]].

## Claims Covered
- The project aims to secure enormous AI compute capacity and establish a manufacturing system that shortens design-test-fab iteration by co-locating logic/memory/mask/test/package workflows.
- It frames logistics feasibility (e.g., large orbital payload throughput) as inseparable from compute strategy.
- It links hardware, launch, and orbital operations as one system rather than separate domains.
