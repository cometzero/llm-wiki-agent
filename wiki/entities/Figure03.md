## Summary
[[Figure03]] is Figure AI's humanoid robot platform as described in the robotics sources. It is notable here for combining electric actuators, hybrid control, stereo vision, palm cameras, fingertip tactile sensors, and battery/cooling improvements with the [[Helix02]] behavior model.

## Key Points
- Serves as the hardware platform for Helix-enabled autonomy and dexterity demos.
- Adds palm cameras and tactile sensing that support in-hand perception and precise force control.
- Is used as evidence that modern humanoids are becoming quieter, more stable, and more capable of real-world manipulation.
