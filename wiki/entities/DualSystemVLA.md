## Summary
[[DualSystemVLA]] is the family of VLA systems that separates slow multimodal reasoning from fast planner or controller execution. In the study note it is framed as a more realistic way to fit language-rich reasoning into safety-critical driving systems.

## Connections

- [[EndToEndVLA]] — the competing integrated architecture family.
- [[ClosedLoopEvaluation]] — dual-system designs still need closed-loop proof, not just reasoning quality.
- [[Safety]] — the separation is motivated by latency and safety constraints.
