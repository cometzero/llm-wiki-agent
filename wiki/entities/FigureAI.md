## Summary
[[FigureAI]] is the robotics company behind the Figure humanoid platform and the Helix control stack. In the current corpus it is presented as pursuing general-purpose humanoids for household and workforce use through vertically integrated hardware, sensing, simulation, and learned control.

## Key Points
- Builds [[Figure03]] and the [[Helix02]] autonomy stack.
- Emphasizes end-to-end full-body control, tactile manipulation, and long-horizon autonomy.
- Appears to rely on large-scale simulation and modular real-time control infrastructure.
