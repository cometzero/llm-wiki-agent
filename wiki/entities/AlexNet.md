[[AlexNet]] is the convolutional neural network associated with the 2012 ImageNet breakthrough that helped establish GPU-accelerated deep learning as the dominant approach in computer vision. In this wiki it marks the historical transition from hand-engineered features to learned representations trained with sufficient data and compute.

## Connections
- [[ImageNet]] — benchmark where the breakthrough occurred.
- [[GPU]] — compute substrate that made the training practical.
- [[DeepLearning]] — representative early success case.
