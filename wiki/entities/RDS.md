# RDS (Raw Driving Scenarios)

OmniDreams mid-training에 사용된 대규모 자율주행 데이터셋.

## Scale
- 16,600 hours
- 3M 20s clips
- 7 synchronized camera
- 15 countries
- 30 FPS, 1080p

## Camera Configuration
- Front-wide
- Front-telescope
- Front-left/right
- Rear-left/right
- Rear-tele
