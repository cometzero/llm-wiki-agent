## Summary
[[EndToEndVLA]] is the family of VLA systems that integrates perception, reasoning, planning, and action generation inside one multimodal model. The study note presents it as the direct path from scene understanding to executable driving output.

- [[DualSystemVLA]] — the alternative design that separates reasoning and fast execution.
