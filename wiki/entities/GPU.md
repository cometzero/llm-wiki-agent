[[GPU]] is a throughput-oriented parallel processor used to accelerate workloads with high data-parallel structure, especially deep learning. In this wiki it is contrasted with [[CPU]] on latency vs throughput, and connected to [[CUDA]], [[SIMT]], [[TensorCores]], and memory-hierarchy behavior.

## Connections
- [[CPU]] — latency-oriented contrast.
- [[CUDA]] — programming model used to control GPU execution.
- [[SIMT]] — execution model used by NVIDIA GPUs.
- [[TensorCores]] — specialized matrix-multiply units.
- [[DRAM]] — basis of global memory.
- [[SRAM]] — basis of shared memory and registers.
