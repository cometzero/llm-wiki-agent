## Summary
This source argues that changes to the 2028 Korean admissions landscape make high school choice more consequential, especially because grades and school records matter across regular and early admissions pathways. It recommends prioritizing schools where a student can realistically earn top grades rather than chasing ambiguous prestige. The source also distinguishes strategies by school type, arguing that some schools favor holistic admissions while others push students toward standardized-test-heavy paths.

## Key Claims
- [[CollegeAdmissions2028]] increases the importance of grades and school records across multiple admissions pathways.
- [[HighSchoolChoice]] should prioritize the realistic probability of earning top internal grades rather than prestige alone.
- Highly selective specialized schools can still be advantageous, but only when their track record in holistic admissions is strong.
- "Ambiguous prestige" schools may become less attractive because competition is intense while school-record advantages are unevenly distributed.
- Students should manage [[StudentRecord]] strategically and proactively rather than assuming the school will do it for them.
